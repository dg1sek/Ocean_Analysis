# Pre-registration: tide gauges as an independent test of the 70-80 yr line -- written 2026-11-28 before any result

Idea (from the literature search of 28 Nov): sea level is a second, independent instrument of the same ocean. Mediterranean sea level (Trieste, Genoa, Marseille) is reported to follow the AMO/AMOC; Brest (since 1807) gives about three cycles.
Data: PSMSL revised-local-reference annual means (https://psmsl.org/data/obtaining/rlr.annual.data/rlr_annual.zip, downloaded 2026-11-28), stations Brest (id 1), Trieste (154), Genova (59), Marseille (61), Stockholm (78, as a control outside the Atlantic-Mediterranean system with strong land uplift). Valid years only; gaps of up to 3 years filled by linear interpolation; the longest continuous stretch after filling is used. Only complete calendar years up to 2025.
Mediterranean composite: mean of the three station anomalies (each relative to its own mean over 1890-2020), years with at least two stations.

P1 Line at 60-100 yr (alf_tools ML sinusoid with AR(1) errors, quadratic trend): best period, AIC gain, p against 300 AR(1) surrogates. Clear if p < 0.01 (5 series), suggestive if p < 0.05.
P2 Forecast without knowledge of the future (same design as harmonic_test part A, quadratic trend vs quadratic + sinusoid with the period found on training years only; 150 surrogates): cut-offs 1900 (Brest only), 1950, 1975; forecast to the last year of the record. Rule: skill above the 95% null level at >= 2 of the usable cut-offs. The weakness of the quadratic baseline (RESULTS_oos_ssa.md, A2) applies again and is not repaired here.
P3 Phase comparison with SST: with the period fixed at 75 yr, a sinusoid + quadratic trend is fitted over 1890-2025 (common years) to each sea-level series and to the Mediterranean ERSST annual mean (same box as in regional_test). Reported: year of the last maximum of the sinusoid, amplitude, and a 95% interval from 200 moving-block bootstrap replicates (blocks of 5 yr). Sea level integrates heat, so a lag against SST is not a contradiction; only the sign and size of the lag are reported, no rule is attached.
No other tests are added after seeing the results. Land motion (glacial rebound, subsidence) is absorbed by the quadratic trend only; this is a limitation, not a correction.

## Amendment, 2026-11-28, written AFTER seeing the first result (90_sealevel.R)
The rule "longest continuous stretch" gave short series (Brest 1861-1943 n = 83, Genova 1928-1996 n = 69, Marseille 1941-1986 n = 46, Trieste 1901-2024, composite 1928-2024), too short to test a 60-100 yr line; the first result is inconclusive for that reason and is kept (`out/*_stretch.csv`). Added, and labelled as post-hoc:
 P1b/P2b/P3b "gap-aware": all valid years are used without interpolation. For each trial period the quadratic trend + sinusoid is fitted by least squares on the observed years (no AR(1) pre-whitening, because the years are not equally spaced); AIC gain = n log(RSS0/RSS1) - 6. The null comes from 300 AR(1) surrogates generated on the full calendar grid (phi from the residuals of consecutive observed years) and then thinned to the observed years, so the gap pattern is part of the null. Rules (p < 0.01 clear, p < 0.05 suggestive; forecast rule >= 2 usable cut-offs) are unchanged. The composite uses the raw valid values (no filling).

## Part 2 (2026-11-28, written before running): how deep is the heat reservoir seen by sea level? (Alf's mail of 12:15: "Wie behandle ich die Durchmischungstiefe?")
Idea: the thermosteric sea-level change is dh = alpha * H * dT for a water column of effective depth H warming by dT. If SST represents the warming of that column, the slope of sea-level change on SST change (mm per K) gives H = slope / (alpha * 1000), with alpha = 2.3e-4 per K (Mediterranean water, uncertainty about +-20 %; the value is an assumption). Sea level also contains mass, pressure and land-motion terms, so the slope is an apparent depth, an upper bound if those terms correlate positively with SST.
Data: the Mediterranean composite (Trieste, Genova, Marseille anomalies, raw valid values, years with >= 2 stations) and each gauge, against the ERSST Mediterranean annual mean (complete years to 2025).
Method: k-year differences (k = 1, 5, 10; both end years observed) of sea level and SST, OLS with intercept (the intercept absorbs the mean trend); 95% interval from 500 moving-block bootstraps (block length max(5, k) pairs). Also the lag with the strongest correlation of the 1-yr differences within -3..+3 yr (positive = SST leads).
Reported only: slope (mm/K), R^2, implied H (m) with the alpha range. The implied depth is read as a heat-reservoir depth only if the slope interval excludes 0; otherwise "not determined".
No further tests are added after seeing the result.
